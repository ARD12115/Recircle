# Plastic Recycling Guide

## Resin Codes
- **#1 PET** (Water bottles, soda bottles) - Widely recycled
- **#2 HDPE** (Milk jugs, detergent bottles) - Widely recycled
- **#3 PVC** (Pipes, vinyl) - Rarely recycled
- **#4 LDPE** (Bags, wraps) - Store drop-off only
- **#5 PP** (Yogurt cups, bottle caps) - Increasingly recycled
- **#6 PS** (Foam cups, takeout) - Rarely recycled
- **#7 Other** (Mixed, bioplastics) - Check locally

## Preparation
- Rinse containers
- Leave caps ON (they're too small alone)
- No plastic bags in curbside recycling
- No styrofoam
